# Concept brief

**Chosen concept:** Lotus Sanctuary — an atmospheric 3D lake for a short voluntary pause. The product references impermanence, compassion and stillness as artistic themes; it does not claim to reproduce Nirvana or provide a spiritual attainment.

**Three directions considered:** (1) photorealistic temple pilgrimage — too heavy and too doctrinally specific; (2) abstract white-space meditation — elegant but limited exploration; (3) stylized lotus lake — chosen for strong emotional identity and feasible real-time rendering.

**Audience:** international visitors seeking a peaceful exploratory experience. **Success:** users can enter without login, move, interact, pause, and leave freely.
